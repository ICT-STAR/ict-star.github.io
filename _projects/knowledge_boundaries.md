    <p class="kb-eyebrow">KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARIES &amp; UNCERTAINTY</p>
    <h1>From latent knowledge<br>to <em>reliable answers.</em></h1>
    <p class="kb-deck">Why capable models still get facts wrong—and what it takes to make their answers trustworthy.</p>
    <p class="kb-byline">A research thread by STAR Group &amp; collaborators · Eight studies</p>
    <div class="kb-intro">
      <p>A model may know more than it can reliably express, yet be confident about answers that are wrong. Correct answers can hide in the low-probability tail; mistakes can be repeated consistently. External evidence adds another challenge: a model can overlook the right information or be diverted by misleading context.</p>
      <p>We study the journey from knowledge to reliable answers: improving factual recall, assessing uncertainty at the right granularity, and learning how to use evidence or withhold an answer. These are complementary research directions, each addressing a different gap between a model’s capabilities and its behavior.</p>
    </div>
